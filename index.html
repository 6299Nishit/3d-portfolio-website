Digital Stopwatch

Precision digital stopwatch built with Multisim simulation featuring:

  • 74LS47D BCD to 7-segment decoder/driver ICs

  • Minutes and seconds display with 4x seven-segment displays
  • Start, Stop, and Reset control buttons
  • 5.0V VCC power supply
